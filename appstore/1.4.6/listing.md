## Description

```
It sums both channels, so a sound panned to one side isn't lost. Left and right are added at half level each into one mono channel. Taking only the left channel would drop whatever sits on the right.

It's mono, yo! batch converts stereo WAV and AIFF files to mono, for hardware samplers and anything else that wants mono. Some samplers only play mono files, like the original Elektron Digitakt and the Model:Samples. On others, like the Roland SP-404 and the Akai MPC, a mono file takes half the space of a stereo one.

HOW IT WORKS
• Drag in WAV or AIFF files, or a whole folder, or press ⌘O
• Choose an output folder
• Pick the format, bit depth and sample rate
• Press Start

CONVERT A WHOLE SAMPLE PACK
Drop in a folder and every WAV and AIFF inside it is converted, subfolders included, into the same folder structure. Your kicks, snares and loops stay where you put them. There's no file limit.

OUTPUT CONTROL
• Bit depth: 16-bit (default), 24-bit or 32-bit float
• Format: WAV or AIFF
• Sample rate: keep the original, or convert to 44.1, 48 or 96 kHz

FEATURES
• Stereo summed to mono, left and right at half level each
• Multichannel files, like 5.1, folded down with the ITU-R BS.775 weights
• Folder import with the folder structure kept
• Nothing overwritten: if a name is taken, the new file gets a number
• Your original files are never changed
• VoiceOver, Reduce Motion and dark mode
• A small native Mac app

FOR SAMPLERS LIKE
• Erica Synths Sample Drum
• Elektron Digitakt, Model:Samples and Octatrack
• Roland SP-404
• Polyend Tracker
• Akai MPC
• Eurorack sampler modules

PRIVATE AND PAID FOR ONCE
No internet connection, no account, no data collected. Pay once: no subscription, no in-app purchases, no ads. The app is open source under the MIT license.
```

What changed from the live description:
- **It opens on the both-channels point,** in place of "converts stereo audio files to mono, quickly
  and simply".
- **"other gear that requires mono audio files" is precise now.** It names the two samplers that are
  mono-only, the original Digitakt and the Model:Samples, and says what mono does on the others:
  half the space.
- **"Click Convert" is "Press Start",** as the button reads.
- **"Intelligent stereo-to-mono downmix" says what it does:** left and right at half level each.
- **"Eurorack samplers (Assimil8or, Morphagene, etc.)"** becomes "Eurorack sampler modules". The
  description no longer names modules that the site's guides don't cover.
- **New:** originals are never changed, VoiceOver and dark mode, the Octatrack, Tracker and MPC (which
  have guides on the site), and the privacy and pay-once lines.
